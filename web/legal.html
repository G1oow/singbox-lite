Terms of use

Last updated 11 March 2025

Service provided as-is

The measurement service is provided without warranty. Figures reflect the transfer observed during your run and are not a guarantee of sustained capacity, nor a statement about your provider's compliance with any plan.

Acceptable use

Personal and internal diagnostics are welcome. Automated or high-volume use of the measurement endpoints is throttled, and repeated attempts to exceed rate limits may result in temporary blocks.

Access keys

Operator keys are issued to named accounts, must not be shared, and may be revoked if used outside the documented rate limits.

Liability

We are not liable for decisions made on the basis of a measurement, including disputes with a network provider.

Privacy

Last updated 11 March 2025

No account required

You can run a test without registering. We do not build a profile of you and we do not sell measurement data.

What a run records

Server-side, a run records the figures (ping, jitter, download, upload), the node used, the timestamp, and the connecting address as seen by the node. That last item is needed to produce the operator and ASN shown above the dial.

Retention

Aggregate statistics are kept to operate the node network. Per-run records are retained for 30 days and then rolled into aggregates that cannot identify a single visitor.

Your browser

Recent results shown under the dial are stored in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing site data removes them.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. A single functional value is written to local storage to remember your last selected node, which is why the dial opens on the same server next time.

There are no third-party embeds on any page, so no other party receives a request while you use the test.

Changes

Material changes to these terms are noted on the status page and reflected in the date at the top of each section. Questions can go to support@netmeter.example.
