What the numbers mean

Every run reports four figures from the same measurement window.

Measurement notes

Download

Throughput from the node to this browser, measured once the slow-start ramp settles.

Upload

Throughput from this browser back to the node. Most consumer links cap this far below the download figure.

Ping & jitter

Eight sequential probes give the mean round-trip time; jitter is the deviation between them.

Questions

Why is my upload figure so much lower?

Most residential and mobile plans are asymmetric. The upload reading reflects the link you actually have, not the node capacity.

Does the result use all of my bandwidth?

A run opens a small number of parallel streams for about twenty seconds. Other traffic on the same link will compete with it, so close large downloads first for a clean figure.

Is any of this stored?

Recent results stay in this browser's local storage only. Nothing is uploaded and no account is created. See terms & privacy.

Can I automate a test?

Yes — the measurement endpoints are documented on the API page, with rate limits noted there.
